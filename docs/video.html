Transcript

What the video says

  1. A formula can look right, reference real cells, and still return the wrong answer.
  2. That’s why FormulaBench doesn’t stop at valid syntax. It asks whether every target cell is actually correct.
  3. The harness inspects the workbook, builds sheet-aware context, samples Qwen once, then validates exact coverage and formula safety.
  4. It writes atomically, or fails closed, before the organiser-supplied evaluator scores the workbook.
  5. In our public self-evaluation of 400 benchmark tasks, 133 passed: a 33.25% pass rate, with 40.56% cell accuracy.
  6. There were zero missing predictions and zero evaluator errors. But 94.84% of wrong cells sat inside contract-valid workbooks. Running is not the same as right.
  7. After scoring, Tinker lifted development passes from seven to nine. This is checkpoint selection, not held-out proof.
  8. The next step is targeted: deterministic transformations where rules are reliable, plus formula-execution checks where semantics decide the answer.
